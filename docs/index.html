About The Project

For our Data Science and Analytics Capstone project, our team collaborated with our client, Dr. Anca Doloc-Mihu, to analyze real estate in Georgia. Using datasets on healthcare, labor, and crime statistics by county, we explored how these factors impact real estate value. We have analyzed healthcare availability, employment rates, and crime statistics to find potential correlations, offering insights into real estate pricing.

  • Main Dataset: Kaggle Dataset

    This dataset, which was obtained from Kaggle, contains the main statistics we hope to analyze, such as the price of the real estate listing, as well as location.

  • Health Dataset: Healthcare Dataset

    This dataset, which was obtained from Kaggle, contains the main statistics we hope to analyze, such as the price of the real estate listing, as well as location.

  • Unemployment Dataset: Labor Dataset

    While this dataset contained several valuable fields, we decided to explore how unemployment may affect real estate value per county.

  • Crime Dataset: Crime Statistics Dataset

    This dataset contains information relating to crime statistics, including type of crime reported and county.

Results

Explore the results of our key hypotheses

Result 1

Properties located closer to major cities will, on average, be worth more

Result 2

Counties with lower unemployment rates will have higher property values

Result 3

Lower crime and better access to healthcare facilities will result in higher property value

Result 4

Counties with a higher number of technical or government jobs will have higher property values
